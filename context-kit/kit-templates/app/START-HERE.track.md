## 7. Build your app with {{assistantName}}

{{assistantName}} will plan each feature with you and give you steps to check it. Nothing risky happens without asking you.

Start by saying **"plan"** followed by your first idea. Once you approve the plan, {{assistantName}} will build one task at a time and keep an undo history so you can say **"undo that"** if needed.

The `context/knowledge/` folder holds the product description, roadmap, technology choices and project conventions.
